# openSenseMap

The environmental data platform to promote education, environmental and climate protection, enthusiasm for STEM, citizen science, open data, and open source.

## :globe_with_meridians: About

openSenseMap started at the [ifgi (Institute for Geoinformatics, University of Münster)](https://www.uni-muenster.de/Geoinformatics/) and became part of the [senseBox project](https://github.com/sensebox) soon after.
Today openSenseMap is maintained, developed and funded by the non-profit [openSenseLab gGmbH](https://opensenselab.org/) to ensure that it remains freely available to everyone - without fees and without advertising.

## :love_letter: Support Us

Education and free access to knowledge and information are important for positive change in our world. Schools, research institutions, engaged citizens, citizen scientists, journalists and environmental activists worldwide use the free environmental data from the openSenseMap.org platform.

We want to continue to provide freely available data for everyone without fees and without advertising.

Your [donations via betterplace.org](https://www.betterplace.org/en/projects/89947-opensensemap-org-the-free-map-for-environmental-data) are highly appreciated and help keep the platform running.
